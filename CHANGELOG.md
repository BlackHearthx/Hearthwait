# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

Hover timers for everything on the homestead that takes time: mead fermenters,
kilns and smelters and blast furnaces, eitr refineries, spinning wheels and
windmills, cooking stations and ovens, beehives, sap collectors, crops and
saplings, bushes growing back, fires burning down, eggs hatching, young animals
growing up, taming, feeding and pregnancy.

The line sits under the hover text you already read, colored by state: amber
while you wait, green when it is ready, rose when something is about to burn,
gray-blue when the wait is paused.

Paused waits say why instead of showing a countdown that would never move — a
fermenter left in the rain, a windmill with no wind, a furnace out of fuel, a
crop in the wrong biome.

Timers appear on the whole structure, not only on the small ore and fuel mouths.

English and Portuguese included. Client only.
